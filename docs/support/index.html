Help with Move & Pray for iPhone and iPad.

Email andreichev.m@mail.ru with a question, problem, or suggestion.

Common questions

Why did my reminder not arrive?

Enable the reminder in Move & Pray and choose a time. If notification access is disabled, open your device’s Settings, find Move & Pray, and allow notifications. Focus modes and Scheduled Summary can also affect when a notification appears.

There is no reminder for a day when both habits are complete. Reminders are scheduled locally for up to 32 calendar days and renewed when you open the app. If you have not opened it for a month, open it again to renew the schedule.

Does the app need an internet connection?

Marking habits, viewing your calendar, and receiving scheduled local reminders work offline. Opening the support, privacy, or prayer-source links requires a connection.

Where is my history stored?

Your completion marks, reminder settings, and acknowledgement of the prayer guidance are stored on your device. There is no account or app-managed cloud sync. Apple may include the app’s local data in a device backup, depending on your settings.

How can I remove my data?

Use Delete App on your device to remove Move & Pray and its local data. Offloading the app keeps its documents and data, so offloading alone does not erase your history.

Copies in device backups are managed through your Apple backup settings. Restoring a backup may restore app data. The developer does not have a copy of your habit history to retrieve or delete.

What does the prayer checkmark mean?

It is a personal reminder to make time for attentive prayer. It does not measure spiritual progress or replace guidance from a priest. You can reopen the reflection from the app’s main screen.

Which devices and languages are supported?

Move & Pray supports iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 16.6 or later. The app is available in English and Russian.
