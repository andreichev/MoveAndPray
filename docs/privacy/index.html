Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Move & Pray stores your habit history on your device. The app does not send that history to the developer.

This policy covers the Move & Pray iPhone and iPad app, its support website, and related support correspondence. The app is a personal project by the developer known on GitHub as @andreichev.

Data stored in the app

Move & Pray stores the following locally on your device:

  • Movement and prayer completion marks associated with calendar dates.
  • Whether reminders are enabled and the reminder time you choose.
  • Whether you have acknowledged the prayer guidance.

The app uses these records to display your calendar and streaks, show the prayer guidance, and schedule reminders. It has no user accounts, advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs. It does not upload these records to a developer-operated server or provide app-managed cloud sync.

Optional notifications

Reminders are optional and require notification permission. The app schedules them locally using your device’s notification system. Their text can mention movement or prayer and may appear on your lock screen, depending on your notification settings.

You can turn reminders off in the app or change notification permissions and previews in your device’s Settings.

Storage, backups, and deletion

Local history and preferences remain on your device until they are changed or removed. Deleting the app removes its local data. Offloading the app preserves documents and data.

Apple may include app data in iCloud or computer backups, depending on your device and backup settings. Those backups are managed by you and Apple, and restoring one may restore app data. The developer cannot access, recover, or delete your local history or device backups.

Messages to the developer

If you contact support by email, the developer and the email provider receive your email address, message, and any information or attachments you choose to send. This information is used to respond to your request and investigate the issue you report. The app does not automatically attach your habit history.

Support correspondence is stored in the support mailbox. You can contact the developer to ask about or request deletion of that correspondence. Please include only information that is relevant to your request.

This website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. It does not include analytics scripts, advertising, tracking pixels, or cookies set by the developer.

GitHub processes visitor information to operate its hosting service. GitHub states that it logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. This hosting activity is governed by GitHub’s own policies; the developer does not set GitHub’s log retention period. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

External links

The app can open this support website and a prayer-source page on Azbyka.ru in a browser. This website also links to GitHub. When you follow an external link, the destination receives the information normally sent by your browser and handles it under its own policies. The app does not include your habit history in these links.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, this page will be updated to reflect them. The date above identifies the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email andreichev.m@mail.ru. General help is available on the support page.
